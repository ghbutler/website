Net Change in Hospital Prices

Equations 6–7 — Hospital Price Response

% change in Blue Shield's negotiated hospital prices relative to baseline (all insurers present) when is removed. Two channels: premium pass-through (Eq. 6) and insurer leverage shift (Eq. 7).

Eq. 6–7 (Hospital Price Response): $\Delta p = \underbrace{(1-\tau_j) \cdot \text{PremiumEffect}(\Delta\varphi)}_{\color{#1e40af}{\text{pass-through}}} - \underbrace{\tau_j \cdot \text{LeverageShift}}_{\color{#047857}{\text{bargaining gain}}}$

Premium effect: higher premiums raise insurer willingness to pay for hospital access.
Leverage shift: fewer insurers $\Rightarrow$ remaining ones have better outside options $\Rightarrow$ prices fall.

Full specification:  $\Delta p = (1 - \tau_j) \cdot 0.5 \cdot \Delta\varphi \;-\; \tau_j \cdot L$
where $L$ = 9 (Kaiser) or 3 (Blue Cross), reflecting each insurer's leverage significance, and $\Delta\varphi$ is the net premium change from Eq. 5.
