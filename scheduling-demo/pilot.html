PROPOSAL · NOVEMBER 2026

Brooks TA scheduling pilot

Decision requested: identify a Brooks sponsor and IT partner to assess a one-term pilot in three to five course sections. This is a proposed Brooks product, not an approved Cornell service.

Purpose

Help assigned TAs find times for office hours and review sessions with fewer scheduled class conflicts. A single menu lists authorized sections and instructors; selecting one displays aggregate conflicts. The tool does not establish students’ availability.

Bounded pilot

Recruit named TAs and course coordinators; confirm participating sections and the pilot term before using real records. Include all students in those sections and their relevant Cornell course meetings, subject to approval. Keep the existing prototype synthetic until approvals and integrations are complete.

Privacy and security commitments

Use Cornell sign-in with the approved Duo configuration and verified teaching assignments. Authorize every data request on the server. Deliver aggregates only: no names, individual schedules, other-course titles, exports, historical snapshots, or change views. For groups of ten or fewer, the prototype returns conflict/no-conflict rather than percentages; this reduces detail but does not guarantee anonymity. Review small and overlapping groups before live use.

Keeping data current

Propose nightly validated updates with source timestamps and meeting date ranges. Reject incomplete batches; keep the last valid result and warn when delayed. Stop serving results after an agreed freshness limit. Revoke teaching access independently of the data refresh.

Ownership and approval

Brooks names a service owner and technical maintainer. The Registrar/data steward determines permitted use and fields; IT reviews hosting, authentication, security, and the source feed. Agree on retention, incident response, student notice, and a shutdown date before launch. Expansion to other departments requires a separate access and governance review.

Evaluation and exit

Assess TA time saved, usefulness of suggested times, student-reported scheduling problems, data freshness, and support burden. Use voluntary feedback without recording individual schedules or attendance. End access and apply approved deletion rules at the pilot’s end unless continuation is approved.

What is ready

This public GitHub Pages demo demonstrates seeded synthetic cohorts and date-aware conflict maps entirely in the browser. A separate backend prototype tests section authorization, validated batch replacement, and stale-data handling; these controls are not active on this public site. Cornell identity, live enrollment and assignment feeds, unattended nightly operation, approved hosting, and institutional security review remain pending.
