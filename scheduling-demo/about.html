About this demo

For pilot reviewers: Pilot proposal · Technical appendix

A prototype for helping Brooks TAs schedule office hours and review sessions around students’ classes.

Course times are real; student enrollments are synthetic. This is not an approved Cornell service and contains no real student records.

Working in this public demo

  • Reproducible synthetic cohorts. Your browser generates 60 first-year and 60 second-year mock students using seed 20261106, saved public course times, and catalog-based scheduling constraints. All seven concentrations are represented in each year.
  • Local scheduling calculations. The selected section’s conflict map is computed entirely in your browser. No backend, login, student-data feed, or expiration is required.
  • Small-group display. Groups of ten or fewer show conflict/no-conflict instead of percentages. This reduces detail but does not guarantee anonymity in a real-data service.
  • Minimal interface. No names, individual schedules, historical views, or change tracking are displayed. All files and generated mock data are public and inspectable.

Planned for an approved pilot

  • Cornell NetID and Duo. Sign-in through Cornell’s approved authentication service.
  • Verified teaching assignments. Each TA sees only authorized course sections, with access ending when the assignment ends.
  • Automated enrollment updates. An approved feed keeps enrollment and meeting information current.
  • Institutional security controls. Approved hosting, monitoring, retention, incident response, and a named Brooks service owner.

Why can I browse all the sections?

This public demonstration has no authentication or access enforcement. All listed sections are available for review; sections without generated students have no conflict map. The intended operational service would enforce verified teaching assignments on the server.

Interface demonstration, not production security.

A separate backend prototype has synthetic tests for section permissions, batch validation, and stale-data handling. Those controls do not run on this GitHub Pages site. Cornell NetID/Duo, live data, and institutional approval remain pending.

Model limits

Reference week: November 2–6, 2026, Eastern time. The fixed course snapshots and rules were retrieved November 6, 2026. Cohort sizes and concentration shares are illustrative. First-years follow the published fall sample; second-years use the 2025–26 catalog with assumed prior coursework and a summer internship. Full degree completion is not certified: required PUBPOL 5121 was absent from the retrieved terms and no substitution is assumed. Brooks must confirm catalog transition rules.

No scheduled class conflict does not establish availability: work, travel, religious observance, and other commitments are not included.
